About Me:

I’m currently in my second year pursuing a Bachelor's Degree in New Media and Digital Design with a concentration in Commerce and a Marketing minor at Fordham University. My interest in content creation and creativity began early on—I'd make PowerPoint presentations for just about anything, whether it was planning a birthday party or convincing my parents to get me a new car. That early passion for creating has shaped my aspirations to pursue a career in marketing and communications.

Through internships and coursework, I’ve developed skills in building marketing and PR plans, managing influencer outreach, and creating content that connects with diverse and unique audiences.

My dream job is to work in the music, fashion, or travel industry—spaces where I can continue to grow, learn from professionals, and use my creativity and skills to contribute meaningfully to a team.
